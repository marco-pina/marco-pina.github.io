Heterogeneous Agents

From individual income risk to aggregate uncertainty. Each discrete-time example pairs a readable implementation with an efficient solver and one figure. These are teaching examples, with documented simplifications relative to the papers.

  1. 1993 · Huggett

    The Risk-Free Rate in Heterogeneous-Agent Incomplete-Insurance Economies

    Precautionary saving and a market-clearing bond price. Seven income states and Young's method describe the stationary asset distribution.

    IllustrativeEfficientModel notes
  2. 1994 · Aiyagari

    Uninsured Idiosyncratic Risk and Aggregate Saving

    A production economy in which household saving determines aggregate capital, wages, and the interest rate.

    IllustrativeEfficientModel notes

    Also available: continuous-time version.

  3. 1998 · Krusell & Smith

    Income and Wealth Heterogeneity in the Macroeconomy

    Individual employment risk meets aggregate productivity shocks. Households use a simple forecasting rule for aggregate capital, updated until it agrees with the simulated economy.

    IllustrativeEfficientModel notes
